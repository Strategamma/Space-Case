# Visual direction

Futuristic space arcade, approved palette: deep navy, electric cyan, alarming crimson. Original illustrated ship and expressive crew portraits; chunky illuminated terminals, generous spacing and large touch actions. Interface text and icons remain live, accessible elements rather than baked into art.

## Motion

Slow star drift and orbital rotation; staged berth arrivals; station scanner; sealed personnel card reveal; plasma-axe pulse; signal-loss death; expanding victory emblem, light rays, falling confetti and opt-in four-note flourish. Reduced motion disables continuous travel and transforms. No role-specific public night cues.

Phone board uses two columns with a fixed quick-control dock; wider screens expand the berth grid and place Captain controls beside it. Native dialogs preserve focus and Escape dismissal. Death leaves the user in public phases; victory reveals the entire roster and awards team points.

## Exact generation prompts

### Voyage

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original wide background illustration for Space Case, a premium futuristic space arcade social deduction browser game.
Primary request: a beautiful cinematic illustrated research spaceship approaching an enormous eerie crimson cosmic anomaly, in deep midnight navy space with electric cyan engines and small distant stars.
Composition: landscape 3:2, the detailed sleek chunky white and navy research cruiser placed on the right half in three-quarter rear view, its engines glowing cyan, a dramatic red-black eclipse and red gaseous tendrils beyond it in upper center right. Left half is calm darker navy negative space for a live UI title. Lower left contains a small floating illustrated astronaut with cyan helmet rim, tether and orange suit accents, visible full silhouette. Rich hand-painted high-end game key art, clean confident shapes, layered atmospheric depth, tangible spaceship materials, subtle film grain. Sophisticated and playful yet ominous. Restrained luminous cyan and alarming crimson, no purple.
No text, no lettering, no interface, no watermark. Must feel like an original polished arcade game, not a photograph or generic AI galaxy wallpaper.

### Crew atlas

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game crew portrait atlas for Space Case.
Create exactly EIGHT different friendly but suspicious futuristic spaceship crew portraits in a precise 4 column by 2 row contact sheet. Landscape aspect 2:1. Each cell exactly equal size, NO gaps, NO gutters, NO borders. Each portrait stays inside its own cell. Dark navy background per cell. Bust portrait centered in each square, entire head and shoulders visible with generous 12 percent margin. Original high-end stylized illustrated arcade character art, bold confident graphic contours, painterly shading, charming expressive faces with realistic adult proportions slightly stylized, cyan rim lighting, sleek chunky futuristic spacesuits. Richly detailed but readable at 64px. Different ethnicities, ages, genders, hairstyles and expressions. Row 1: confident Black woman with short white hair in orange flight suit; cheerful East Asian man with round glasses in teal science suit; serious South Asian woman with dark braid in off-white engineering suit; older white man with short silver beard in navy commander suit. Row 2: young Black man with curly hair in yellow mechanic suit; brown-skinned woman with shaved side haircut in red navigation suit; East Asian woman with short teal hair in white science suit; bald tan man with a scar and orange utility suit. No helmets hiding faces. No text, symbols, labels, watermarks, interface, props outside cell. Coherent matching style across all eight portraits.


## My orders console

One private surface for card, available action, restrictions/resources and observed-cycle history. Server capabilities determine controls; day/dead/paused/spent states display explanations without target cards. Active action first; basic Hollow skips an empty station-status panel so consensus targets remain prominent. Night countdown stays inside the modal. Hollow bonus order precedes victim selection; consensus locks both. Character/objective are expandable after briefing, while initial reveal shows description directly. Hidden powers never identify their caster through messages or client metadata. Final results/statuses trigger unread dots, not every order edit. At 320px target cards place portraits above names to prevent short callsigns splitting beside avatars; wider phones use compact portrait/name rows. Retain focus and scroll through live updates. Victory closes orders to expose the celebration.
